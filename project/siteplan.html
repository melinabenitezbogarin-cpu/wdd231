Site plan - Be productive not just busy

Site plan Document

Site Name

Site Name: Be productive not just busy

Reason for selection: This name directly reflects the main objective of the project: guiding people to focus on intentional actions, develop daily discipline, and achieve meaningful goals rather than simply filling their schedules to feel occupied.

Site Purpose

Purpose Statement: The website provides practical guides, actionable strategies, and motivational content to help visitors cultivate discipline, establish well-structured personal goals, and overcome procrastination. It serves as an educational hub designed to transform passive online scrolling into intentional daily progress.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • How can I overcome procrastination and stay disciplined when starting a new routine or project?
  • How can I set goals and plans in a correct way to really improve in my life?

Color Scheme

  • Primary Background (#111827 - Dark Slate): Used for the main body background.
  • Secondary Background (#1F2937 - Card Gray): Used for section cards, header, and footer containers.
  • Accent Highlight (#10B981 - Neon Mint Green): Used for section headings, logo highlights, and borders.
  • Secondary Accent (#6366F1 - Indigo): Used for interactive links and secondary accents.
  • Primary Text (#F3F4F6 - Off-White): Used for high-contrast, readable text throughout the page.

Typography

  • Roboto Bold (700): Applied to main titles, headings (h1, h2), and primary action buttons to create strong visual emphasis.
  • Roboto Regular (400): Applied to paragraph content, list items, and standard body text for comfortable reading.
  • Roboto Light (300): Applied to secondary metadata, subtitles, footers, and decorative captions.
